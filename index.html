Privacy Policy

Trigon Puzzle · Developer: Farhan Bashir · Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Trigon Puzzle (package com.farhan.trigonpuzzle, "the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be short and plain.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any account. Your game progress stays on your phone. The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your level progress, stars, points, last leaderboard rank, remaining hints, and sound and vibration settings. This data is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Google may include it in your personal device backup.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads (no ads in levels 1 to 3). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps, and diagnostics such as crash data from the ads SDK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Elsewhere, you can opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Leaderboard and player profile (Google Play Games)

The app has an optional world leaderboard powered by Google Play Games Services. If you sign in with Play Games, Google processes your Play Games player ID, gamer name (display name) and profile picture, and your total game points, so your rank can be shown. Your gamer name, picture and points are visible to other players on the public leaderboard. You can control your profile visibility and gamer name in the Google Play Games settings. Signing in is optional; the game can be played fully without it. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Rewarded videos

When you choose to watch a video to receive extra hints, the video is an ad served by Google AdMob as described in section 2. Watching is always optional.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not use precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app has no chat and no user accounts of its own. The only information other players can see is your Play Games leaderboard entry (gamer name, picture and points), and only if you sign in.

7. Children

Trigon Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies. To limit ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, there is no account to delete; you may still contact us with any request.

9. Security

Data sent by the Google AdMob and Google Play Games libraries is encrypted in transit. Game progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Use the in-app privacy choices for ads, your Android ad settings, or contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
